| **Data Deletion Requests** | < 48 Hours | Automated token purge + account deletion |

## 2. Self-Service User Operations
- **Account Disconnection**: Navigate to `/social-accounts`, click **Disconnect** to immediately purge social access tokens.
- **Subscription Cancellation**: Navigate to `/settings`, click **Cancel Subscription** to schedule cancellation at the end of the billing period.
- **Data Deletion**: Submit formal request on `/data-deletion`.
