# GrowthPilot AI — Production-Ready Multi-Platform Social Media Growth SaaS

**GrowthPilot AI** is an enterprise-grade AI social media growth, content automation, analytics, and marketing platform purpose-built for:
1. **Instagram** (Professional Accounts, Reels, Carousels, Stories, Insights)
2. **Facebook** (Pages, Videos, Reels, Community Posts, Page Insights)
3. **LinkedIn** (Pages, User Profiles, B2B Thought Leadership, Institutional Analysis, Lead Gen)
4. **TikTok** (Direct Video Publishing, TikTok for Business, Creator Analytics)

---

## 🌟 Core Architecture & Capabilities

### 1. Unified Social Command Center
* **Combined Audience Tracking**: 77,400+ verified followers across Instagram (24,850), Facebook (12,430), LinkedIn (8,920), and TikTok (31,200).
* **Multi-Platform Analytics Filter**: Global 1-click switcher between *All Platforms*, *Instagram*, *Facebook*, *LinkedIn*, and *TikTok*.
* **Real Reach, Views & Engagement**: 642k+ Reach, 1.28M+ Video Views, 98.4k+ Interactions, and 218 Direct Leads.

### 2. AI Social Growth Score (0–100)
Calculated across **9 distinct algorithmic pillars**:
1. Content Consistency
2. Organic Engagement Rate
3. Non-Follower Audience Reach
4. Authentic Follower Growth Rate
5. Content Quality & 3-Second Hook Retention
6. Platform Posting Cadence & Frequency
7. Audience Response Time & Sentiment
8. Profile Optimization & SEO
9. Conversion Performance & Pipeline Attribution

### 3. Cross-Platform AI Content Studio ("Create Content Everywhere")
* Generates tailored, distinct platform variations from a single topic or campaign goal:
  * **Instagram**: Visual hook, 30s Reel script, carousel slides, save & share drivers, curated hashtag clusters.
  * **Facebook**: Conversational discussion hook, long-form community post, video script, link previews.
  * **LinkedIn**: B2B executive hook, structured bullet points, financial/ROI underwriting analysis, corporate CTAs.
  * **TikTok**: 3-second visual & verbal pattern interrupts, 30–45s vertical video script, on-screen text timeline.
* **1-Click AI Content Optimizer**: Scans draft content and boosts score to 95+/100 by adding algorithm retention triggers.
* **Multilingual Engine**: Native support for English, Arabic (العربية), Urdu (اردو), Hindi (हिन्दी), Spanish, and French.

### 4. Real Estate Growth Mode
Specialized workflow for property developers, brokers, and investment funds:
* **Inputs**: Developer, Project, Location, Typology, Price, Payment Plan, Handover, Amenities, Investment ROI.
* **Auto-Generated Assets**: 30s Cinematic Reel walkthrough script, Facebook project overview, LinkedIn institutional underwriting model, TikTok 1% monthly payment plan video, and high-converting lead ad creative.

### 5. Content Calendar & Dynamic Best Posting Times
* **Platform-Specific Peak Windows**:
  * Instagram: **7:30 PM** (Tue / Thu) — Peak evening save velocity
  * Facebook: **8:00 PM** (Wed / Sun) — Highest community comments
  * LinkedIn: **9:00 AM** (Tue / Wed) — Executive commute & morning planning
  * TikTok: **9:30 PM** (Daily) — Algorithmic binge distribution
* **Auto Growth Modes**:
  * `OFF`: All automated actions paused
  * `MANUAL`: AI assists upon user request
  * `SEMI-AUTOMATIC`: AI creates and optimizes; User approves before publishing
  * `AUTOMATIC`: AI publishes via authorized official platform APIs at peak windows
* **Emergency Master Kill-Switch**: 1-click `"PAUSE ALL AUTOMATIONS"` button.

### 6. Competitor Radar & AI SWOT
* Track public handle cadence, format distribution, and engagement benchmarks.
* AI automatically extracts **Strengths, Weaknesses, Content Gaps, Opportunities, and Counter-Strategies**.

### 7. Unified Lead Center CRM
* Tracks social lead acquisition from Meta Lead Ads, LinkedIn InMail forms, and TikTok Instant forms.
* Full sales pipeline stages: `NEW`, `CONTACTED`, `QUALIFIED`, `MEETING`, `NEGOTIATION`, `CONVERTED`, `LOST`.
* Deal valuation, notes history, and 1-click CSV export.

### 8. Weekly AI Executive Reports
* PDF-ready weekly executive audit breaking down follower surges, top-performing formats, best lead platforms, and next week's strategic content roadmap.

## 🔒 Security, Compliance & Anti-Abuse Commitment

GrowthPilot AI operates in strict accordance with official developer policies:
* **Zero Fake Engagement**: No bot followers, no click farms, no artificial likes or views.
* **100% OAuth 2.0 PKCE**: Passwords are never requested or stored.

## 🚀 Quick Start Guide

### 1. Install Dependencies
```bash
npm install
```

### 2. Configure Database & Environment
```bash
# Push schema to SQLite/PostgreSQL
npx prisma db push
npx prisma generate
```

### 3. Run Development Server
```bash
npm run dev
```
Open [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser.
